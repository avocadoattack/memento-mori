  Wrap the number in a <span> with:
    display: inline-block
    min-width: 2ch        /* 2 characters wide minimum — handles 0–99 */
    text-align: center
    font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums
    font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1

eliminating the side-to-side movement.
