**Current:** `X days spent unconscious`  
**New:** `X% of your life you're unconscious`

**Calculation:** `(sleepHoursPerNight / 24 * 100).toFixed(1)` — this is the fixed daily fraction, no lifetime total needed. Display as e.g. `"33.3% of your life you're unconscious"`.

## Summary of Calculation Changes

| Card | Old calc | New calc |
|---|---|---|
| Sleeping perspective | `rawHours / 24` (days) | `sleepHoursPerNight / 24 * 100` (%) |
| Working perspective | `workHours / 2080` (work-years) | `workHoursTotal / 8760 * 12` (months) |
| School stat (yrs) | sum of `.years` from active levels | `schoolHoursTotalInstructions / 8760` |
| School perspective | `schoolHours / 3500` (PhD programs) | `schoolHoursTotalInstructions / 100` (100h projects) |
| Commute perspective | `commuteHours / 40` (NY→LA trips) | `commuteHoursTotal / 2` (podcasts) |
| Streaming perspective | `streamingHours / 30` (Netflix series) | `streamingHoursTotal / 1200` (languages fluent) |

All other calculations (eating days, chores days, gym visits, books) remain unchanged — only the display text is being updated for those.
